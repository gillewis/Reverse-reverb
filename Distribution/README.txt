REVERSE REVERB 1.0 - by Gil Lewis

INSTALL
Open "Reverse Reverb 1.0.0 Installer.pkg", then restart your DAW.
(macOS 11 or later, Apple Silicon and Intel.)

If macOS warns it "cannot check" the installer: click Done, open
System Settings > Privacy & Security, and click "Open Anyway".

USE
1. Put Reverse Reverb on any track.
2. Drag in a clip (Cubase/Nuendo timeline) or an audio file.
3. Set the Length (1-7 seconds).
4. Drag the swell into your project. Line up its END with the START
   of the sound.

Renders are saved in Music/Reverse Reverb Renders.
